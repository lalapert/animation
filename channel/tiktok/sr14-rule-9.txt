Analog horror archive, tape 14. Night guard Leon Marsh, December 9, 1999. The night shift rules on the guard office wall stop at rule 8. Under them is a rule 9 in blue pen, in his own handwriting. It is 2:10 A.M. He sat down to write in the night log that he did not write it. His hand kept going. Found footage from his camcorder and CAM 02.
