## 2. How to make a toon episode

### 2.1 Folder
Copy the template folder to `<prefix><NN>-<slug>/` (Void Mart: `vm03-dennis/`), without `out/`, `voice/out/` and `.venv`; `ln -s ../.venv-daily <folder>/.venv`; copy over effects you reuse from earlier `sfx/out/`. Do not edit old episodes.

### 2.2 Writing (the part that decides views)
- **Write the script file first** (owner, 11/7): `channel/scripts/<PREFIX><NN>_<slug>.md` from `channel/scripts/TEMPLATE.md` (example: `VL03_help_wanted.md`). It has three parts:
  1. **The English-speaking-audience check** (US/UK): score every row 1–5 with reasons, weakest points first (hook, premise in one line, relatability, laugh density, natural English, no insider/Korean/copyright references, ending loop, comment bait; the horror table for tapes). Under 32 in total, or any row at 1–2: rewrite and score again before producing. Say whether it leans American or British, the biggest risk and its fix, and **three title candidates from three different formulas in `.claude/skills/ig-reel/hooks.json`**, scored with `python3 -I .claude/skills/ig-reel/hookscore.py --hook "<title>"`. Pick the funniest one that names something concrete (a number, a name, an object); the score is advice, not a gate (it was built on talking-head hooks, `channel/IG_SKILLS.md`).
  2. **The script table**: id, who, the emotion direction as an eleven_v4 audio tag, the English line (exactly the subtitle; ` / ` for a subtitle line break), and the action direction (what each character does, `expr`, hands always with their arms, the shot, sound and music). Stage-only rows have no id.
  3. Easter egg, season thread, running gag.
  4. **The post**: the caption (format in §3.4) with its two search terms, and the hook card if the episode has one.
- Then `python3 channel/tools/script_to_lines.py channel/scripts/<file>.md <folder>` writes `voice/lines.json` from the table (tags in `text`, the line in `plain` and `sub`); the picture follows the action column. If production changes a line, change the script file too, so it stays the record.
- Report the audience-check total and the weakest row in the final message.
- English only, everywhere (`channel/tools/check_english.py`). No Korea-specific culture, places or names.
- **Variety rules (owner, 11/8):** every Volt Labs invention is a different kind of thing and looks different (the tripod ray is episode 1 only; old inventions appear later as shelf props); no ending type repeated within 5 episodes; no animal gags (pigeons and cats were overused; background cameos of 1–2 s at most); at least 2 movement beats per episode, and about one episode in three takes the cast outside (lab: backyard, rooftop, street; Void Mart: aisles, stockroom, parking lot).
- **How they move (owner, 11/8: options 1+2+6):** walking legs look stiff, so we do not show them. (1) Waist-up travel: the camera follows the character from the waist up, the body dips at each footfall and rises between (`walkBob`/`travel`/`drawTraveller` in `channel/brand/travel.js`), footsteps on the beat (`footfalls`). (2) Something in front hides the legs: the lab bench (`LAB_STATIC.front`), the store counter (`STATIC.front`), the aisle shelf (`AISLE.front` in `channel/brand/sets.js`), fences, cars. (6) Two-feet hops for small bouncy moves (`hopPose`; Pip's head pops over the shelf). Full-body walking (`channel/brand/bodies.js`, `drawFull`) only in wide shots where the person is small. **11/12 owner (final):** walking stays exactly as above (the half-hidden ways shown on 11/8). The full-body walk samples (`channel/brand/legs.js`, `channel/brand/anatomy.js`) are parked: do not use them in episodes. Quick exits: off frame, then the next shot. Check these in the audience table before recording.
- **0–2 s: the hook is a picture.** Open on the weird customer or the weird thing, never on greetings or explanations.
- **Hook card (experiment, 4 episodes: VL4, VM5, VL5, VM6):** from frame 1 to 2.5 s, one English card of 6 words or fewer at the top (y 250–400, inside the Reels/Shorts safe zone), so a muted scroller gets the situation before the first line. Judge it at the 12/7 audit against the episodes without one; drop it at once if the owner does not like it.
- Subtitles stay inside x 120–960 (the button rail covers the right edge): `subtitle()` in vmkit.js shrinks a line wider than 840 px.
- **At least 3 laughs in 45 s, at least one of them visual** (something you get with the sound off).
- Deadpan Rae (rules), delighted Pip (kindness makes it worse), MAGS (every disaster is a sales opportunity); one-off customers carry the episode's idea.
- End on a button (the incident counter, a MAGS line, a sip) that loops into the first frame.
- Length 40–60 s; up to 75 s only for big story episodes (midpoint, finale).
- Season threads and easter eggs: `SEASON1.md` §3, `LAB_SERIES.md` §2, `UNIVERSE.md` (one egg every 2–3 episodes per series, never explained).
- US calendar: Thanksgiving 11/26, Black Friday 11/27, Christmas Eve, New Year's Eve. 10:00 KST (owner 11/12; Metricool's best hour for both accounts) is 8 PM the evening before on the US East Coast and 5 PM on the West Coast, so Void Mart 11 *Leftovers* (11/27 KST) lands on Thanksgiving evening, Void Mart 12 *Restock* (11/29) the day after Black Friday, Void Mart 25 *Holiday Hours* (12/25) on Christmas Eve evening, Volt Labs 10 *The Turkey Ray* (11/26) on the evening before Thanksgiving, Volt Labs 11 *Doorbuster* (11/28) on Black Friday evening, Volt Labs 24 *Naughty List* (12/24), Volt Labs 28 *Resolutions* (1/1) on New Year's Eve evening.

### 2.4 Voices, sound, music
- Cast and settings: `channel/voices/VOICES.md`. Rae is Kar2 pitched 1.5 semitones down (the `pitch` field in `voice/lines.json` does it). Timings that depend on a word inside a line (a cut-off, a countdown) are read from that line's word timings in `voice/out/<id>.json`, never hard-coded. One-off customers: an ElevenLabs stock voice, never the same one two episodes running. Model `eleven_v4`.
- **Emotional acting is required (owner, 11/7).** Every line gets an eleven_v4 audio tag in front, in `text`: `[gasps, delighted]`, `[pleading, defensive]`, `[heartbroken wail]`, `[furious, shrill]`, `[flustered, embarrassed] ... [softly, shy] ...`. Big for Pip, Vera, MAGS and the one-off characters; small for Rae and Cleo, whose deadpan is the joke (`[flatly]`, `[bored]`, `[deadpan]`, `[tired sigh]`). Keep the plain line in `plain` and the subtitle in `sub` (the tools strip tags from subtitles and lip sync). Settings: Pip stability 0.3 / style 0.65, Vera 0.3 / 0.7, MAGS 0.45 / 0.5, one-offs 0.4 / 0.5, Rae 0.6 / 0.2, Cleo 0.6 / 0.25 (`voices/VOICES.md`).
- **Watch for stray words.** Tags sometimes add a word ("Oh,", "No!") or read the neighbouring line. `python3 tools/voice_eleven.py` → `python3 tools/asr_eleven.py` must exit 0: it compares only the spoken words, so any extra or changed word shows as BAD. Retake a BAD line once with a different tag; if it is still wrong, drop the tag for that line and loosen the settings instead. An `asr` field only for a correct read spelled differently (Rae/Ray).
- `tools/timeline.py`: gaps before each line (comic timing), shots, events; subtitles never overlap (it asserts).
- Effects: `sfx/prompts.json` → `python3 tools/sfx_eleven.py <new names>`; reuse earlier ones.
- `tools/score.py`: the show's jazzy bed (contrabass pizzicato walk, brushes, vibraphone), silence under punchlines, a xylophone button at the end; MAGS always gets the glockenspiel jingle. Balance with `tools/score_gain.json`.
- `node tools/render.cjs events`, `tools/mix.py`, `node tools/render.cjs video 3`, `sh tools/mux.sh out/video.mp4 out/mix.wav <folder>.mp4`, `tools/srt.py`, `poster.jpg`, `CREDITS`, `build.sh`.

## 4. When something fails

- No Metricool tools in the session: still make, check, commit and push; log `ready, not scheduled (no Metricool connector)` with the media URL; say so first in the final message.
- One fix round per problem. If a check still fails, do not schedule; log it and say so.
- ElevenLabs credits under 5,000: reuse effects, keep retakes to one, say so in the log. The account is shared with the owner's other work, so the account total moves on its own: log this episode's own use (characters of its takes), not the change in the total.
- Never change environment settings, permissions or other scheduled tasks; never delete posts (to cancel, set `draft: true` and tell the owner).
