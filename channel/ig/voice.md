## Who we are

- **Name:** Void Mart (Instagram name field: `Void Mart | animated sitcoms`)
- **Handles:** YouTube @thevoidmart, Instagram voidmartshow
- **What we make, in one sentence:** Two short animated sitcoms in one universe: Void Mart (the night shift at the last convenience store before the edge of space) and Volt Labs (a broke mad scientist and her deadpan assistant).
- **Who we are talking to:** English-speaking adults 18–34, mostly US, who have worked retail, food service or a bad first job, and who watch adult-friendly cartoons on their phone (but our content is PG, no swearing).

- **Faceless:** yes, animated characters only. Voices are characters, not a narrator.

## What we sound like (captions, titles, descriptions)

- **Tone:** dry, deadpan, a little tired, like a coworker texting you from the night shift. The joke is understatement.
- **Three captions that sound like us:**
  - "A tiny customer tries to pay with a star. Then a moon. Rae has a policy for that."
  - "Corporate sent a self-checkout. It never sleeps. It never smiles. Rae has notes."
  - "Day one of world domination. The Adoration Ray works. Technically."
- **Words we use:** shift, register, customer, policy, corporate, manager, rent, evil (casual), technically, again.
- **Words we never use:** "epic", "insane", "you won't believe", "wait for it", "must watch", "stop scrolling", "follow for more", "tag someone", any em dash in captions, anything Korean.
- **Swearing:** no. **Emoji in captions:** never.
- **Pace (dialogue):** about 170 wpm, with timed pauses for the joke.
